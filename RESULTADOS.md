```sql
-- Q1: Top 10 pickup zones by trips in the assigned month, and their change vs the same month of the previous year.
-- Source: marts.agg_zone_hour (pre-aggregated: scans far fewer rows than fact_trips).
-- Grouped by location_id because zone names are not unique.
with by_zone as (
    select
        pu_location_id,
        sum(case when year = ${YEAR} then trips end) as trips_assigned,
        sum(case when year = ${CMP_YEAR} then trips end) as trips_comparison
    from marts.agg_zone_hour
    where month = ${MONTH} and year in (${YEAR}, ${CMP_YEAR})
    group by pu_location_id
)
select
    z.borough,
    z.zone as pickup_zone,
    b.trips_assigned,
    b.trips_comparison,
    b.trips_assigned - b.trips_comparison as trips_change,
    round(100.0 * (b.trips_assigned - b.trips_comparison) / nullif(b.trips_comparison, 0), 2) as trips_change_pct
from by_zone as b
inner join marts.dim_zone as z on z.location_id = b.pu_location_id
order by b.trips_assigned desc nulls last
limit 10
```

```sql
-- Q2: Hour of the day with the highest average revenue per trip (revenue = total_amount), for each of the two months.
-- Source: marts.agg_zone_hour; average per trip = sum(total_revenue) / sum(trips).
with by_hour as (
    select
        year,
        month,
        pickup_hour,
        sum(trips) as trips,
        round(sum(total_revenue) / sum(trips), 2) as avg_revenue_per_trip
    from marts.agg_zone_hour
    where month = ${MONTH} and year in (${YEAR}, ${CMP_YEAR})
    group by year, month, pickup_hour
),
ranked as (
    select by_hour.*, row_number() over (partition by year order by avg_revenue_per_trip desc) as rank_in_month
    from by_hour
)
select year, month, pickup_hour, trips, avg_revenue_per_trip
from ranked
where rank_in_month = 1
order by year desc
```

## 3. Share of trips with cbd_congestion_fee > 0 in the assigned month, and the 5 pickup zones with the highest share.

```sql
-- Q3: Share of trips with cbd_congestion_fee > 0 in the assigned month, and the 5 pickup zones with the highest share.
-- Zones need at least 1,000 trips in the month so a zone with a handful of trips cannot top the list at 100%.
-- First row = all zones; next rows = top 5 zones.
with by_zone as (
    select
        f.pu_location_id,
        count(*) as trips,
        sum(case when f.cbd_congestion_fee > 0 then 1 else 0 end) as trips_with_cbd_fee
    from marts.fact_trips as f
    where f.year = ${YEAR} and f.month = ${MONTH}
    group by f.pu_location_id
),
overall as (
    select
        0 as sort_order,
        'ALL ZONES' as scope,
        cast(null as varchar) as borough,
        sum(trips) as trips,
        sum(trips_with_cbd_fee) as trips_with_cbd_fee,
        round(100.0 * sum(trips_with_cbd_fee) / sum(trips), 2) as pct_with_cbd_fee
    from by_zone
),
top_zones as (
    select
        row_number() over (order by 1.0 * b.trips_with_cbd_fee / b.trips desc, b.trips desc) as sort_order,
        z.zone as scope,
        z.borough,
        b.trips,
        b.trips_with_cbd_fee,
        round(100.0 * b.trips_with_cbd_fee / b.trips, 2) as pct_with_cbd_fee
    from by_zone as b
    inner join marts.dim_zone as z on z.location_id = b.pu_location_id
    where b.trips >= 1000
)
select scope, borough, trips, trips_with_cbd_fee, pct_with_cbd_fee
from (
    select * from overall
    union all
    select * from top_zones where sort_order <= 5
) as result
order by sort_order
```

```sql
    a.trips as trips_assigned,

inner join by_month as c on a.year = ${YEAR} and c.year = ${CMP_YEAR}
```

## Conclusiones

- La demanda de taxis amarillos crece con fuerza en enero 2025 (+15,8 % de viajes, +12,7 % de recaudación). El crecimiento se concentra en el núcleo de Manhattan (Midtown, Upper East Side, Union Sq), mientras los aeropuertos se estancan o bajan.
- El peaje de congestión (CBD, US$ 0,75 desde el 5 de enero) alcanza a 3 de cada 4 viajes desde que rige. Varias de las zonas que más crecen están dentro de la zona de peaje (Union Sq +24,1 %, Times Sq +17,1 %, Midtown Center +16,9 %): en este mes no se observa una caída de demanda donde se cobra. Es una correlación, no una medición de causa.
- El viaje promedio es algo más corto (3,16 contra 3,29 millas) y más barato. La baja no viene de las tarifas: la mediana sigue en US$ 12,80, y sin Flex Fare la tarifa por tramo de distancia es casi igual. Viene de la mezcla, con más Flex Fare (tarifa fija acordada por app) y menos aeropuertos y viajes largos. La propina con tarjeta, en cambio, no se mueve (alrededor del 26 %).
- Los promedios son frágiles. Un único viaje con una tarifa de US$ 863.372 cambia el signo de la tarifa media de aeropuertos y el segundo puesto de las horas, por eso se reportan también la mediana y el promedio sin atípicos. La siguiente regla de calidad a agregar es un tope de tarifa o de tarifa por milla.
- Los datos de 2025 traen cambios de formato que el pipeline absorbe sin romperse: la columna nueva cbd_congestion_fee (NULL en 2024) y el aumento de Flex Fare sin passenger_count (warning R05). Las cifras salen de los marts de dbt en Snowflake, son reproducibles con make all MES=2025-01 y los dos meses reconcilian.
